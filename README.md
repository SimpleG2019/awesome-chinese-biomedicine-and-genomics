## 🔬 Core Focus Areas
- 🔬 **Cell & Gene Therapy**: Autologous & allogeneic CAR-T/CAR-NK, base editing, prime editing, non-viral vector delivery.
- 💊 **Novel Oncology & Bispecific ADCs**: Bispecific antibodies, antibody-drug conjugates, synthetic lethality targets.
- 🧬 **Genomics & Spatial Transcriptomics**: High-throughput single-cell sequencing, multi-omics atlas, clinical metagenomics.
- 🏥 **NMPA Clinical Trials & IITs**: Investigator-Initiated Trial outcomes, Phase I-III endpoints, companion diagnostics.
